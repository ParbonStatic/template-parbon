# Festival Theme Presets & Cultural Blueprints

Use these curated design palettes, typography pairings, multi-day chapter structures, and interactive ritual concepts when scaffolding a new festival tribute site.

---

## 1. Deepavali / Diwali (Festival of Lights)
- **Palette**:
  - Primary (`gold`): `#D4AF37` (light `#F5E08E`, bright `#FFD700`, dark `#9E7B1C`)
  - Secondary (`marigold`): `#FF8F00` (light `#FFB300`, deep `#E65100`)
  - Accent (`vermilion`): `#B71C1C` (light `#D32F2F`, dark `#7F0000`)
  - Surface (`midnight`): `#1A0B2E` (deep `#0F051D`, card `#24113E`, soft `#311852`)
  - Text (`ivory`): `#FFFDF8` (warm `#F7EFE1`, muted `#E2D4BA`)
- **Fonts**: `Rozha One` (display), `Cinzel Decorative` (accent), `Playfair Display` (serif), `Poppins` (sans)
- **5 Chapters**: Day 1 Dhanteras, Day 2 Naraka Chaturdashi (Choti Diwali), Day 3 Lakshmi Puja, Day 4 Govardhan Puja / Annakut, Day 5 Bhai Dooj.
- **Interactive Ritual**: Light a Virtual Clay Diya + Golden Spark Burst + Blessing Card Generator.
- **Sacred Verse**: *Pavamana Mantra* (*"Asato Ma Sadgamaya, Tamaso Ma Jyotirgamaya, Mrityorma Amritam Gamaya"*).

## 2. Durga Puja / Sharadotsav (Autumnal Invocation of Shakti)
- **Palette**:
  - Primary (`kashGold`): `#E0A96D` / `#D4AF37` (Dhakai Zari Gold)
  - Secondary (`sindoor`): `#C62828` (Alta & Sindoor Crimson)
  - Accent (`shiuli`): `#FF7043` (Shiuli / Parijat Coral Orange)
  - Surface (`mahogany`): `#1E0A10` (deep `#120408`, card `#2B0F18`)
  - Text (`kashWhite`): `#FFFDF9` (Kashphool Ivory)
- **Fonts**: `Rozha One` or `Yatra One` (display), `Playfair Display` (serif), `Poppins` (sans)
- **5 Chapters**: Maha Shashti (Bodhon & Amantran), Maha Saptami (Nabapatrika Snan), Maha Ashtami (Kumari Puja & Sandhi Puja), Maha Navami (Maha Aarti & Dhunuchi Naach), Vijaya Dashami (Sindoor Khela & Visarjan).
- **Interactive Ritual**: Offer Virtual *Anjali* (Hibiscus & Shiuli Petals) / Light the *Dhunuchi* Incense Burner + Subho Bijoya Card Generator.
- **Sacred Verse**: *Ya Devi Sarvabhuteshu* (*Devi Mahatmyam*).
